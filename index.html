    About

    Midnight Scholar is a tool built to cut through the noise of dense documents. Whether it's a 40-page PDF report or a scanned photo of notes, it extracts the core text and vital points so you can read smarter, not harder.

    How it works

    Everything runs directly in your browser — there is no server. PDFs are parsed for text with PDF.js; images are run through Tesseract.js OCR. The extracted text is then analyzed and condensed into a summary and key takeaways based on the depth you choose. No uploads ever leave your device.
